Meet Megan

building design and interiors,
designed as one.

Design Well Studio is led by Megan Miner — a residential building designer and interior designer with over fifteen years of experience transforming homes across the Bay Area. Megan began her career in interior design, then followed her passion for construction into custom homes and building design. That combination is genuinely rare in this industry, and it's the foundation of everything we do.

Most firms treat blue prints and interior design as separate services. We never have. From the first line of your floor plan to the final fabric selection, every decision is made with the whole home in mind — so your exterior and interior always feel like they belong to each other. And to you.

Our Signature — Live Drafting™

Most designers disappear for weeks, then reappear with a concept you may or may not love. We do something different. During our Live Drafting sessions, you sit alongside Megan — in person or virtually — while she draws your floor plan or 3D rendering in real time. You see every decision as it's made. You give input. Changes happen instantly. No waiting, no guessing, no surprises.

It's behind-the-curtain access to the design process that no one else offers — and clients tell us it's the part they didn't know they needed until they experienced it. It cuts design time significantly, builds real trust, and makes the whole thing genuinely fun.

"Let the home speak for itself."

Megan Miner

Founder · Residential Building Designer · Lead Interior Designer
